Common questions

Why did the alarm not sound when the app was closed?

On Android, allow notifications and exact alarms when the operating system requests them. On iPhone, allow notifications and keep the selected alarm sound available in the app. Battery-saver and Focus settings can also limit delivery.

How do tasks work?

Typing and shake are free tasks. Steps, call, random and one-tap fallback tasks may require two short rewarded ads after the seven-day introductory period. Math, memory and barcode tasks are SleepRise Pro features. A task must be completed before the alarm can be dismissed.

What is the seven-day trial?

SleepRise may offer a seven-day introductory period when the relevant monthly or yearly product offer is configured in Apple App Store Connect or Google Play Console. After the trial, the selected store subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in the store settings. The exact price and currency are shown by the store before purchase.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Open the Pro panel and choose Restore purchases or Manage subscription. Apple subscriptions are managed in Apple ID subscription settings; Google subscriptions are managed in Google Play subscription settings. SleepRise cannot cancel a store subscription on your behalf.

Why does SleepRise ask for microphone or camera access?

Microphone access is requested for the optional sleep-analysis feature. Camera access is requested for the optional barcode wake-up task. Both features require an explicit user action, and you can deny access and continue using the rest of the app.
