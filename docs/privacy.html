Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026 · Last updated: 18 August 2026

EnglishTürkçe özeti aşağıdadır

This Privacy Policy explains how SleepRise (“SleepRise”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the SleepRise mobile application. SleepRise is a sleep and wake-up assistant. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Information you choose to provide

SleepRise may store the alarms, labels, task settings, relaxation preferences, language choice, sleep notes and locally generated sleep-session information that you enter or create. These settings are stored on your device unless a feature explicitly says otherwise. We do not require an account to use the core alarm features.

When you use sleep analysis, the app may request microphone access. The microphone is activated only after you start the feature. The app uses the signal for the requested analysis and does not intentionally upload or retain raw audio for a user profile. When you use barcode tasks, the camera is activated only after you start scanning; the barcode value needed for the task is stored locally on the device.

Advertising and third-party services

SleepRise uses Google AdMob for limited advertising outside the initial trial period. AdMob and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, approximate location and advertising-related signals according to their own policies and your platform choices. The app does not place a banner over the alarm controls; banner advertising is limited to the Relax area and is hidden in cinema or fullscreen mode.

SleepRise uses Apple App Store and Google Play Billing through the native purchase layer for optional monthly and yearly SleepRise Pro subscriptions. We do not receive your full payment card number. The relevant store processes payment, manages renewal, and provides the purchase status needed to unlock Pro.

SleepRise uses native local notifications for alarms and optional reminders. Notifications are scheduled on your device. You can change notification permission in the operating-system settings.

How we use information

We use local information to operate alarms, enforce task completion, render sleep insights, save your preferences, restore purchases and provide support when you contact us. We do not sell personal information. SleepRise does not claim to measure clinical sleep stages; estimated stages and scores are presented for wellness and routine-building purposes only.

Children

SleepRise is a general-audience wellness application. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Most SleepRise data is stored locally and can be removed by deleting alarms, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app. Advertising and subscription providers may retain information under their own policies. For a privacy question or deletion request involving a support message, email us at ali5921592@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can disable microphone, camera, notification and advertising-related permissions from your device settings where supported. You can manage or cancel subscriptions from the Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings. The initial seven-day in-app trial is available only when the corresponding store offer has been configured for the product and is subject to the store’s terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when we publish a material revision.

Contact

For privacy and data questions, contact ali5921592@gmail.com.
