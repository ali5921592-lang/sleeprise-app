## App Store Connect — English

### App name

SleepRise

### Subtitle

Sleep well. Wake ready.

### Promotional text

A calmer alarm, relaxing soundscapes, and wake-up tasks for a better morning.

### Description

SleepRise helps you create a calmer night and a more intentional morning. Set a focused alarm, choose a wake-up task, relax with sound and atmosphere, and review simple wellness insights about your routine.

Wake up with intention. Choose typing or shake for a free wake-up task, or use optional Pro missions such as math, memory and barcode challenges. The alarm remains active until the selected task is completed. Volume Down provides one single-use snooze; it is not an unlimited escape from the task.

Wind down in Relax with breathing guidance, sound mixes, a sleep timer and cinematic atmospheres. During the first seven days, SleepRise is designed to let new users explore the core flow without ads or feature gates. When a store introductory offer is available, SleepRise Pro can remove ads and unlock Pro features. After the introductory period, the store subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

SleepRise Pro features include ad-free Relax and atmospheres, math, memory and barcode wake-up missions, your own video uploads, and unlimited sleep analysis. Custom alarm sounds remain available to all users. The exact price, currency, introductory offer and renewal terms are shown by the store before purchase.

SleepRise is a wellness and routine-building app, not a medical device. Sleep insights are estimates and should not be used to diagnose or treat a health condition. The optional scheduled-call feature is a personal safety aid only; it must not be used to impersonate emergency services, real people or official organisations.

SleepRise supports Turkish, English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.

### Keywords

sleep,alarm,wake up,relax,breathing,sleep timer,soundscape,bedtime,routine,wellness

### Review notes

Review the core flow without purchasing: choose English, continue through onboarding, open Alarm, choose typing or shake, and tap Test now. To review Pro, use the sandbox/TestFlight or Google Play test account with the configured subscription product. The app includes a visible Later button on the Pro sheet, a Restore purchases action, and a Manage subscription action. SleepRise does not claim clinical sleep-stage measurement. Microphone and camera permissions are requested only after the related feature is started.

## Subscription disclosure block for both stores

SleepRise Pro is an auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription. The introductory offer, if eligible and configured for the current store product, lasts seven days at no charge. At the end of the introductory period, the subscription renews at the price and billing period shown by Apple App Store or Google Play unless cancelled before renewal. Users can restore purchases and manage or cancel the subscription from the app store settings. SleepRise does not receive full payment card details.

## App review and release checklist

The store product IDs are `com.sleepify.app.pro.monthly` and `com.sleepify.app.pro.yearly`. Before submission, create the subscription group and official seven-day introductory offers in App Store Connect and Google Play Console, add the exact current localized prices, and test Restore purchases and Manage subscription with sandbox accounts. The app’s screenshots in `store-assets/` are descriptive marketing compositions that use the real UI and do not include browser chrome.
